- The journal's stated editorial sequence first asks whether a result, if
  correct, is worth publishing, then emphasizes understandable/coherent
  exposition and correctness.
- Elsevier's current submission workflow permits editable LaTeX source and
  converts submission files for review.
- Initial-submission reference formatting is flexible, provided references are
  complete and consistent; DOI metadata is strongly encouraged.
- The public RiE material accessible during N6 did not establish a
  journal-specific double-anonymized-review requirement. Do not create a
  double-blind package unless the live submission system requests it.
- Current Elsevier policy requires disclosure of substantive generative-AI use
  in manuscript preparation. The manuscript now contains that declaration.
- Simultaneous submission is prohibited by Elsevier publication ethics.
