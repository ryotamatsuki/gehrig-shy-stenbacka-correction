# Stage 7 institutional evidence log — 2026-09-20

## Purpose

Validate the economic plausibility of the GSS core primitives without using
real-world evidence to overstate the theorem's external validity.

## Evidence class 1 — data/history-conditioned pricing

### Federal Trade Commission

FTC surveillance-pricing materials document the use or potential use of
consumer data such as location, browsing behavior and shopping history for
individualized or targeted pricing.

Primary sources:

- https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/features/surveillance-pricing
- https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-surveillance-pricing-study-indicates-wide-range-personal-data-used-set-individualized-consumer
- https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/08/ftc-seeks-comment-enforcement-policy-statement-regarding-personalized-pricing

Use in paper:
supports the plausibility of history/type information entering pricing.

Do not infer:
that entrants lack equivalent information, or that the exact GSS information
structure describes any specific current market.

## Evidence class 2 — existing/new customer pricing

### Financial Conduct Authority

FCA general-insurance reforms target tenure-based "price walking" and compare
renewal pricing with equivalent new-business pricing.

Primary sources:

- https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-confirms-measures-protect-customers-loyalty-penalty-home-motor-insurance-markets
- https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/icobs6b/icobs6bs2
- https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/corporate/ep25-2.pdf

Use in paper:
supports the institutional relevance of customer tenure/status as a pricing
dimension.

Do not infer:
that the regulated insurance game is strategically equivalent to GSS.

## Evidence class 3 — switching frictions

### Ofcom

Communications-market evidence documents contract tenure, possible
early-termination charges, differences between in- and out-of-contract prices,
and active switching behavior.

Primary sources:

- https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/saving-money/in-or-out
- https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/bills-and-charges/pricing-and-consumer-engagement
- https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/switching-provider/1.6-million-brits-hit-switch-on-their-broadband-provider

Use in paper:
supports switching friction as an economically relevant primitive.

Welfare caution:
a monetary termination charge may be a transfer rather than a social resource
loss. GSS `sigma` is most naturally mapped to real disutility such as time,
hassle, learning, compatibility, or nonrecoverable switching effort when P4 is
interpreted as a social-welfare result.

## Overall institutional verdict

`SUPPORTED PRIMITIVES / NO STRUCTURAL VALIDATION CLAIM`.

The institutional evidence is adequate for motivation and interpretation. It
does not warrant claiming that the GSS model is a calibrated representation of
any one market.
