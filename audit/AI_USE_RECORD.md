## Tool

- Service: OpenAI ChatGPT
- Developer: OpenAI
- Model/version: varied across project sessions. A single fixed model version is not part of the replication path.

## Manuscript-preparation uses

ChatGPT was used supportively for:

- language editing and readability;
- content organization;
- source and citation checking;
- submission-guideline checks;
- internal consistency checks.

These uses are disclosed in the separate declaration immediately before the references.

## Research-process uses

ChatGPT was also used supportively for:

- mathematical consistency checking;
- exploration and checking of candidate unilateral deviations and exact counterexamples;
- drafting and review of verification code;
- reproducibility-workflow and audit documentation.

These uses are disclosed in the manuscript's Verification methodology and reproducibility section, rather than being represented as mere language editing.

## Human oversight and evidentiary boundary

AI output is not treated as proof, data, or independent evidence. Proof-critical claims are checked against the model primitives and executable artifacts. Exact symbolic/rational checks are reproducible in Python/SymPy. Selected threshold reductions, domain containment, welfare identities/signs, and exact rational counterexamples are separately formalized and kernel-checked in Lean 4. Source attributions and bibliography metadata are independently checked.

The economic interpretation, scope restrictions, and final manuscript content remain the author's responsibility.

## Reproducibility

Reproduction of the paper's reported mathematical results does not require access to ChatGPT. The replication package and formal artifacts are the reproducibility objects.

## Documentation retention

The repository retains the audit trail, verification scripts, formal artifacts, workflow records, and manuscript revisions. Chat transcripts/prompts are not asserted to be a complete archival record of every AI interaction. If an editor requests further documentation, available project records should be supplied without overstating completeness.

## Privacy / confidentiality / tool-terms check

Elsevier requires authors to check AI-tool terms so that privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and publication rights are protected, including avoiding grants of unrelated reuse or model-training rights over unpublished manuscript material.

Repository automation cannot inspect the author's account-level AI data controls or retrospectively certify the terms/settings governing every project session.
