The exact equation-level correction remains anchored to the complete 14

## Important citation-genealogy observation

Gnutzmann (2014) states that GSS (2011) constrains the entrant to uniform
pricing and that allowing the entrant to discriminate reverses the GSS social
welfare result. This is conceptually distinct from the present correction:
the present project keeps the GSS strategy set and shows that the published
welfare headline already reverses **inside that same model** after correcting
consumer surplus.

The 2016 Shy–Stenbacka–Zhang paper likewise treats GSS (2011) as an established
benchmark for exclusive incumbent discrimination rather than as a corrected
result.

## Application-neutral absorption test

The source pricing game can be stripped to the following canonical object:

> a one-dimensional price choice against a weighted sum of two clipped affine
> demand segments, with segment-specific horizontal shifts; under HBP the
> incumbent has two separable segment prices while the entrant has one common
> price.

Searches found general work on Hotelling equilibrium, multi-segment demand, and
piecewise price competition, but no located theorem was a direct citation-ready
replacement that simultaneously:
- maps to the exact GSS asymmetric information/price-instrument structure;
- supplies the source's missing global-validity conditions; and
- contains the welfare correction.

The active-set algebra itself should therefore **not** be marketed as a new
general theorem. Its role is source-specific certification of where the
published formulas are valid.
