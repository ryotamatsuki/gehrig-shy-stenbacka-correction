# Stage 0 — Idea / Motivation Intake

## Objective

Determine whether the certified audit findings in Gehrig, Shy & Stenbacka (2011) define a precise correction-paper question rather than a collection of local errata.

## Economic starting point

The source compares history-based price discrimination (HBP) with uniform pricing in a Hotelling market with an incumbent, an entrant, inherited consumers with switching costs, and new consumers.

The source's substantive claims include consumer-surplus, entry, incumbent-profit, and total-welfare comparisons. The clean-room audit found that the welfare headline and the global-equilibrium interpretation of the displayed HBP prices cannot both be retained as written.

## One-sentence research question

> Within the Gehrig–Shy–Stenbacka (2011) model, what is the correct welfare comparison between history-based and uniform pricing, and on exactly what parameter domain do the published interior pricing formulas constitute a global Nash equilibrium?

This question is falsifiable both by prior art and by direct equilibrium analysis.

## Candidate mechanisms / correction objects

### A. Welfare-accounting correction

The source's consumer-surplus integration appears to omit a factor of three. Because the source's incumbent-profit difference is smaller than the corrected consumer loss, the total-welfare ranking reverses.

### B. Global-equilibrium correction

The displayed prices solve the simultaneous interior FOCs. They need not be global best responses once demand is correctly clipped to `[0,1]`. The entrant can profitably cross an active-set boundary and serve only the new cohort for admissible parameters even when both displayed cohort shares at the candidate are strictly interior.

### C. Complete-correspondence extension

If analytically tractable, a correction can replace an unqualified interior-equilibrium statement with exact active-set conditions or a complete pure-price equilibrium correspondence. This is potentially more valuable than merely appending a counterexample.

## Candidate contribution hierarchy

**Headline:** corrected welfare sign and exact global-equilibrium scope.

**Secondary:** complete equilibrium correspondence, if it survives Stage 4/4A and is not absorbed by known Hotelling/behavior-based-pricing results.

**Ancillary:** the prose-level sign mistake in interpreting `Delta c=c_A-c_B`.

## Kill tests applied

- This is not merely a parameterization exercise: the welfare sign reverses and a displayed equilibrium can fail globally.
- This is not merely a typographical correction: the affected statement is Result 5 and the abstract/conclusion welfare headline.
- The project will be killed or downgraded if the correction is already published, if the factor-three discrepancy is only a version artifact, or if the global-equilibrium issue is an immediate already-known correction of this exact paper.
- A complete-correspondence extension will be dropped if it adds length without changing the correction's mathematical or economic content.
