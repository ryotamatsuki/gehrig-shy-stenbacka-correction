## Version relationship and substantive comparison

The 14 December 2009 draft predates the 2011 VOR and has the same title,
authors, model, and headline claims exposed by the VOR abstract. The accessible
VOR metadata states that uniform pricing gives higher consumer surplus, entry is
invariant, and the incumbent’s profit gain exceeds the consumer loss. Because
the VOR PDF could not be opened, an equation-by-equation VOR/draft comparison
cannot be certified. The audit therefore labels the frozen 2009 mathematical
source explicitly and does not silently treat the inaccessible VOR as inspected.

The later same-author paper “A Welfare Evaluation of History-Based Price
Discrimination,” *Journal of Industry, Competition and Trade* 12(4), 2012,
373–393, is a different asymmetric-duopoly model in which both firms can price
discriminate. It is not a version of this entry paper and is not substituted for
the source frozen here.
